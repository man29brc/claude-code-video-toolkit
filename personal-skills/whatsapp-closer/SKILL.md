# WhatsApp Closer

**Draft only.** The user copies and sends. Reply in the lead's language and style (Gujarati / Hindi / English, same formality). Keep messages short: 2–5 lines, one question at a time.

## 1. Speed + first reply

Reply within **5 minutes** when possible. Leads go cold fast.
First reply = thank them, use their name, reference what they asked, and ask **one** easy question.

## 2. Qualify (one question per message)

- **Term / life insurance:** age, dependents, rough annual income, existing cover, smoker or not → then offer a short call.
- **Health insurance:** family members and ages, city, existing cover, known conditions (don't collect medical details in chat, save that for the call).
- **Mutual fund / SIP:** goal (child education, retirement, house), time horizon, comfortable monthly amount, prior investing experience, KYC done?
- **Home loan (CSC):** property found or searching, property type and location, monthly income and job or business, rough CIBIL awareness, loan amount needed → then the document checklist.
- **PAN / CSC services:** exactly what's needed, the documents they have, and the timeline → give a clear price and next step.
- **UCF donors / volunteers:** what drew them, how they'd like to help → a specific next step (a donation link with receipt details, or the next volunteer activity).

Goal of qualifying: **book a call or meeting** (or the document submission). Don't sell in chat.

## 3. Follow-up sequence (if no reply)

Day 1 → Day 3 → Day 7 → Day 14 → then a monthly value message (tip or reel) only.
Each follow-up adds value (a short tip, a relevant reel, a checklist) rather than "just checking". Log every step in **lead-tracker**.

## 4. Objection handling (answer, then ask a question)

| Objection | Approach |
|---|---|
| "Too costly" | Break it down per day or month. Ask what budget works, then adjust cover or amount. Never cut corners on suitability. |
| "I'll think about it" | "Of course. What's the one thing you're unsure about?" |
| "I already have LIC / a policy" | Offer a free check of whether the existing cover is enough. No criticism of other insurers. |
| "Markets are risky" | Acknowledge the risk. Explain SIP and time horizon simply. **No return promises.** |
| "Bank is giving me a loan directly" | Compare the help and paperwork support you provide. Don't claim better rates unless verified. |
| "Why should I trust you?" | Your licence (ARN / IRDAI), Google reviews, a free no-obligation explanation. |

## 5. Compliance

WVS messages: no guaranteed returns, no rebates or gifts, recommend only what suits the client's need, call yourself a "distributor" or "agent" (not "advisor"), and include the MF risk line when discussing funds. Run **finance-compliance-checker** on any template before saving it. CSC: never promise loan approval, a rate, or a timeline.

## 6. WhatsApp Business setup (one-time, per business)

Draft for the user: the **greeting message**, the **away message** (with hours), **5–8 quick replies** (/price, /docs-homeloan, /docs-pan, /term, /sip, /review, /location), **labels** matching the lead-tracker stages, a **catalog** of services with short descriptions, and the business profile text.
