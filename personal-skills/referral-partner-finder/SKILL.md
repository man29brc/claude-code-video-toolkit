# Referral Partner Finder

Home-loan buyers talk to **property dealers and builders first**. A few good partners can bring steady, high-value leads without depending on shop walk-ins.

## 1. Pick the target (per business)

| Business | Best partners |
|---|---|
| **CSC — home loans** | Property dealers or brokers, builders and site sales offices, CAs and tax consultants, interior or construction contractors |
| **WVS — insurance & MF** | CAs and tax consultants (tax-season clients), HR or owners of small firms (employee cover or SIPs), other non-competing professionals |
| **UCF — NGO** | Company CSR teams, schools and colleges, housing societies, religious or community groups, local businesses |

## 2. Find candidates

- Use web search for Vadodara listings (e.g. "property dealers Gotri Vadodara", "new residential projects Vadodara 2026", "CA firms Alkapuri"), plus builder websites and project pages.
- The user adds names from their own contacts and Google Maps. Claude can't browse Maps directly.
- Build a list of 15–20 with: name, type, area, why they fit, public contact channel, and source link. **Only publicly listed business contacts.** Never scrape personal data.

## 3. The offer, within legal limits

- **WVS (insurance / MF):** ⛔ **never share commission** with unlicensed people. IRDAI and AMFI prohibit it. Offer value instead: free insurance or SIP awareness sessions for their clients or staff, a co-branded tax-season checklist, fast service for their referrals.
- **CSC home loans:** referral payouts depend on the lender or DSA agreement and must follow it. Put any payout **in writing** and keep it transparent. Otherwise offer fast document support and loan-status updates for their buyers, which makes their sales easier.
- **UCF:** CSR alignment, impact reports, volunteer days, recognition.

## 4. Outreach (draft only, the user sends)

For each partner, write a short WhatsApp or email message plus a **one-page intro** (who you are, how you help their customers, proof, contact):
- Line 1: something specific about them (their project or area).
- Line 2: the benefit to **them** ("your buyers get loan documents sorted in X days").
- Line 3: a small ask ("10-minute call this week?").
Follow up on Day 3 and Day 10, then stop.

Prep for the meeting: 5 questions to ask them, what to bring, and how the referral handoff works (a WhatsApp group or a shared form).

## 5. Partner tracker

Add a **"Partners"** tab to the **Lead Tracker** sheet:
| Partner | Type | Area | Status (Found/Contacted/Meeting/Active/No) | Referrals sent | Won | Last contact | Next step |
Log referred leads in lead-tracker with Source = "Referral partner: <name>".

Review monthly: keep feeding the partners who send leads, and drop the rest.
