# Google Profile Manager

People searching "home loan agent near me", "PAN card centre Vadodara" or "term insurance agent Vadodara" are **ready to act**. This is the cheapest source of high-intent local leads.

There's usually **no connector** for Google Business Profile, so produce **ready-to-paste** content and click-by-click steps. The user posts it.

## 1. One-time profile audit (per business)

Ask for screenshots of the profile, or the details, then check and fix:
- **Primary category** + 2–4 secondary categories (e.g. CSC: the most accurate categories for the services actually offered; WVS: "Insurance agency" / "Financial consultant"-type categories that match the licence. Never "Financial planner" if not registered as an adviser).
- **Business name:** exactly the real name. No keyword stuffing (against Google policy).
- **Description (750 chars):** what you do, for whom, area served (Vadodara plus nearby areas), trust points, and how to contact. WVS: include "AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor" and no return promises.
- **Services list** with a short description each (home loans, PAN, and the other CSC services; term life insurance, SIP / mutual funds).
- Hours, phone, WhatsApp link, website or Instagram link, and service area.
- **Photos:** logo, cover, and 10+ real photos (office or shop, documents process, team). Add 1–2 new photos a week.

## 2. Weekly routine (about 15 minutes)

Each week, for each business, produce:
- **2 Google posts** (Update or Offer): ≤1,500 characters, the first 100 characters carry the message, one CTA button (Call / WhatsApp / Learn more), and one natural local keyword. Reuse the week's Reel topic.
- **1 Q&A:** a real common question plus a clear answer. The user posts it from their own account and answers it from the business account.
- **Review replies:** use the trust-builder skill for these.
- **1–2 new photos:** suggest what to shoot.

WVS posts must pass **finance-compliance-checker**. CSC home-loan posts: no promised approval, rates or timelines.

## 3. UCF profile setup

Guide the user step by step: create the profile (an NGO or charity category that fits), use a service area if there's no public office, add a clear mission description, photos of real activity (with consent), and a donation or volunteer link. Verification may need a phone, video or postcard. Follow Google's current flow.

## 4. Monthly check

Ask for **Performance** screenshots (searches, calls, website clicks, direction requests, and the search terms people used). Report what changed and adjust the keywords and post topics. Log calls and chats that came from Google in **lead-tracker** with Source = Google profile.

## Rules
- No fake reviews, no review incentives, no keyword-stuffed names. These can get the profile suspended.
- Only real addresses and real photos.
- No health insurance posts (it's sold only via CSC Balaji on request).
