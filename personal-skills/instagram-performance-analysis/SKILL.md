# Instagram Performance Analysis

The goal is **decisions, not dashboards**: what to do more of, what to stop, and what to test next.

## 1. Scope

- **Which business / account?** WVS, CSC Balaji, or UCF.
- **Period:** default last 30 days, compared with the 30 days before.
- **Goal** the account is judged on: enquiries (profile visits, link clicks, DMs), reach/followers, or (for UCF) awareness and donations.

## 2. Get the data (use the first route that works)

1. **Connected analytics tools.** If a marketing-data connector is available (e.g. Porter Metrics with Instagram/Facebook Insights), use it: list the connected accounts, and pull per-post metrics and account metrics for the period. If the account isn't connected, tell the user how to connect it in that tool and continue with route 2 or 3 meanwhile.
2. **Scheduler analytics.** If Buffer, Social Post, or another scheduler connector exposes post metrics, use those.
3. **User-provided data.** Ask for screenshots or an export from **Instagram app → Professional dashboard → Insights** (or Meta Business Suite → Insights → Content, export CSV). Useful: the last 10–20 Reels/posts with views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves, follows, and posting date/time, plus the account overview (reach, followers, profile activity).

Always say which data source you used and what's missing. **Never invent or estimate numbers you weren't given.**

## 3. Analyse

Per post, compute where possible:
- **Engagement rate** = (likes + comments + shares + saves) ÷ reach
- **Share rate** and **save rate** (strongest signals of content people value)
- **Follows per 1,000 reach** (did it grow the account?)
- For Reels: hook strength, if retention/skip data is present

Then look for patterns across the top 20% vs the bottom 20%:
- Format (Reel / carousel / photo), length, topic/pillar, hook type, language, face on camera or not, posting day and time, trending audio vs original.
- With fewer than ~10 posts, call patterns "early signals", not conclusions.

## 4. Output

```
## <Business> · Instagram · <period> vs <previous period>
Data: <source> · Missing: <gaps>

**Headline:** <one sentence — the single most important finding>

| Metric | This period | Previous | Change |
|---|---|---|---|

**Top 3 posts:** what they were · key numbers · why they likely worked
**Bottom 3 posts:** what they were · likely reason

**What's working:** 2–4 bullets
**What's not:** 2–3 bullets

## Next month
1. Do more of: …
2. Stop / change: …
3. Test: <one experiment, with how to measure it>
**Best posting slots (from the data):** …
```

Offer to save the key learnings to memory, and to feed them into the content-calendar skill.

## Rules
- Correlation isn't causation. Say "likely" when inferring why.
- Compare like with like: Reels vs Reels, carousels vs carousels.
- Treat the three businesses separately. Never combine their numbers.
