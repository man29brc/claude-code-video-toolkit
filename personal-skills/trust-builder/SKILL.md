# Trust Builder

The user's core problem: **people don't trust them yet.** Proof beats promotion. Every completed service is a chance to earn a review.
The shop counter is **not** used for this. Everything happens **online, via WhatsApp**.

## 1. After every completed service → review request

Triggers: a policy issued, an SIP started, a home loan sanctioned or disbursed, a PAN card delivered, any CSC service completed, a donation received or event held (UCF).

Draft a WhatsApp message for the user to send (**draft only, the user sends it**), in the customer's language (Gujarati / Hindi / English):
- Personal thank-you with their name and the service
- One line: "It would really help me if you shared your experience on Google. It takes 30 seconds."
- The business's **Google review link** (ask the user for each business's link once and keep it in memory. It's in Google Business Profile → "Ask for reviews".)
- Send within 24 hours of completion. One polite reminder after 3 days, then stop.

**Never** offer money, discounts or gifts for reviews, never ask for only 5-star reviews, and never write reviews for customers. It breaks Google policy, and for WVS, rebate rules.

## 2. Testimonials (for content)

Ask happy customers for a 2–3 line text or a short voice note: "What was your problem, and how did it go?"
- Get **clear consent** to use it publicly, and ask if they want their first name only, initials, or anonymous.
- **WVS:** no return figures, claim amounts or "guaranteed" language. Run **finance-compliance-checker** before using a testimonial in any post, and **ask the insurer** before using testimonials in ads.

## 3. Turn proof into content (faceless)

- **Quote card** (Canva / instagram-carousel): the quote + first name + service. No photos of people without consent.
- **Story reel** (story-reel-maker): "How we helped a first-time home buyer in Vadodara get their documents ready" with anonymised details.
- **Instagram Highlight "Reviews"**: screenshots of Google reviews with personal details blurred.
- **Milestones**: "25 families helped with PAN cards this month" (only real numbers).

## 4. Reply to every Google review

Draft replies within 24 hours:
- Positive: thank them by name, mention the service, one warm line. Add a natural keyword ("home loan in Vadodara") where it fits.
- Negative: calm, no argument, own what's fair, and take it offline ("Please WhatsApp us at … so we can fix this").

## 5. Weekly trust score (for weekly-mentor-checkin)

Report: reviews requested vs received per business, current Google rating and count, and testimonials collected.
